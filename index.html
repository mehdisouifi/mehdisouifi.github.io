Slow Craftworks YouTube Channel Upload Tool

A private tool that publishes finished films to the Slow Craftworks YouTube channel. One channel, one user, no sign-up.

What it does

  • Uploads a finished video file to the channel and sets its title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Adds the video to the right playlist.
  • Reads the channel's own statistics — views and watch time — so its owner can see how a film performed.

It runs on the owner's own computer, using the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, after the owner approves access through Google. It touches no other account and no other channel.

The channel

Slow Craftworks publishes long-form films of leatherwork made by hand in Barcelona. No narration and no music — the knife, the thread and the hammer. Each film follows one object from pattern to finished piece.
