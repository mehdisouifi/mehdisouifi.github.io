Slow Craft Works

Long-form films of leatherwork made by hand in Barcelona. No narration, no music — the knife, the thread and the hammer.

Every film follows one object from pattern to finished piece: cutting, skiving, edge work, glueing, saddle stitching, burnishing. Nothing is sped up past the point where you could follow it.
