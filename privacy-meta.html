Privacy Policy

Slow Craftworks Facebook and Instagram Publishing Tool · last updated 16 October 2026

This policy covers "Jarvis Publisher SC", the private tool used to publish videos to the Slow Craftworks Facebook Page and the @slowcraftworks Instagram account. The tool has a single user: the owner of those accounts. It is not offered to anyone else and has no sign-up.

What it accesses

With a token the owner created in Meta Business Suite for their own Page, the tool uses the Facebook and Instagram APIs to:

  • post the owner's own videos, with titles and captions, to the Page and to the Instagram account;
  • read the Page's and the account's own statistics, such as views and watch time.

It accesses no data belonging to any other person, Page or account.

What it stores, and where

The access token is stored on the owner's own computer, readable only by the owner. Statistics are stored in a private repository belonging to the owner. Nothing is stored on a server run by this site, because there is no such server: these pages are static files.

What it does not do

  • No data about visitors, followers or commenters is collected, sold, shared or transferred to anyone.
  • No advertising, tracking, profiling or analytics of visitors.
  • No cookies are set by these pages.

Deleting the data

Because the tool holds no personal data about anyone other than its owner, there is nothing to delete for visitors. The owner can withdraw the tool's access at any time in Meta Business Suite, which invalidates the stored token.
